OUR STORY

Built by people who live in their cars.

We commute, we haul, we road-trip. So we got tired of cheap car gadgets that rattle, slip, or do not fit, and built a shop that stocks only the gear we would keep in our own cabins.

HOW IT STARTED

A small crew, one rattling glovebox too many.

We are a small crew that spends real time behind the wheel. The daily driver, the weekend haul, the long haul out of state. Somewhere between a mount that drooped at the first pothole and a "fits all vehicles" caddy that fit none, we got fed up with gear that looks the part online and quits the moment it hits the road.

So we changed how we shop. Now nothing gets listed until it rides in a real car for real miles. We mount it, load it, charge from it, and drive it through the kind of week our customers drive. If it rattles, slips, or does not fit the way the box promised, it never earns a spot in the cabin. What is left is a short, honest catalog of gear that holds up.

01

Road-tested before it is listed

Every pick gets driven before it gets sold. We run it in daily drivers and on real road trips, then keep only the gear that earns its place in the cabin.

02

Honest specs, no theatre

We list what a thing actually does. Real wattage, real fit, real limits. No invented ratings, no fake scarcity, no claims we cannot stand behind once it is on your dash.

03

Free U.S. shipping over $50

Orders over $50 ship free within the U.S. Smaller orders show a flat rate at checkout before you pay, so the number you see is the number you pay.

04

14-day returns

You have 14 days from delivery to send something back. Start a return from your order email and we send return instructions. If a pick does not earn its spot in your cabin, it goes back.

THE ROAD-TESTED PROMISE

If it does not earn its spot, we do not sell it.

Our promise is simple and it is the whole reason the shop exists. Every pick is driven before it goes live. The catalog stays short on purpose, because the only gear here is gear we would keep in our own cars.
