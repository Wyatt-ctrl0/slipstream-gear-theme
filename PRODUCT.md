# PRODUCT.md — Slipstream Gear

register: brand

## Product purpose
Slipstream Gear is a Shopify storefront for clever gadgets and gear for the daily driver: car organizers, detailing tools, phone mounts, fast chargers, dashcams, trunk storage, comfort, and road-trip kit. The promise is curation, every pick is road-tested before it earns a spot in the catalog. This repo is the design itself (a storefront), so the visitor's impression IS the deliverable.

## Users
People who spend real time in their vehicles and want the small annoyances of driving solved: commuters, road-trippers, rideshare and delivery drivers, anyone whose cabin is a second living space. Practical buyers who distrust cheap gadgets that rattle, slip, or do not fit.

## Brand voice (three words)
Fast, practical, driver-literate. Confident without hype. Talks like someone who actually lives in their car and has tested the gear on real commutes and long hauls.

### Voice guardrails (enforced, do not regress)
- No em-dashes in customer-facing copy. Use periods, commas, colons, or "and".
- No false or unverified claims. No fake scarcity, no invented guarantees, no fake reviews. Reviews are honestly deferred ("Reviews open after launch"); the promise stands on policy.
- Avoid filler: vibrant, crafted, meticulously, elevate, game-changer, revolutionary.
- Auto nouns over generic ones: the cabin, the trunk, the dash, the daily driver, the long haul, the glovebox, the center console.

## Aesthetic lane
"Big type, speed slash" performance-automotive. Oversized uppercase display, a skewed red speed-slash, cinematic dark stages, velocity/aero motion (streak reveals, headlight sweep, count-ups). Reference points: motorsport liveries and taillights, not luxury-car minimalism. The red reads like a taillight, not a luxury accent.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI SaaS landing pages (centered hero, three icon cards, gradient text).
- Luxury-auto minimalism (navy + chrome + thin serif). Wrong register.
- The category reflex "auto = dark blue + silver". This brand is asphalt-black + racing red.
- Any garden / pet / rescue residue from the theme's ancestry (Tend & Tool, SnagNest, Molly & Sophie).

## Strategic principles
- WCAG 2.1 AA is a floor, never regress it. The racing-red palette has known text-contrast traps (see DESIGN.md).
- Motion is always reduced-motion gated and has a no-JS reveal safety net.
- The speed-slash is the hero's single statement; other motion stays supporting.
